03 – Chuyện cũ, vị xưa

What Does "No Nê" Mean?

No Nê is a Vietnamese expression that describes more than just being full. It's the feeling of satisfaction, comfort and joy after a good meal, when your stomach is full and your heart is happy.

Our restaurant is inspired by the bao cấp era, when life in Vietnam was simple, food was precious and meals were always shared with warmth and laughter. No Nê was not created to chase modern glamour, but to keep those memories alive.

Here, people don't just eat to be full. They eat to remember.
